# v2.0.0-beta2 — keep your data in Google Drive, and an icon

Second 2.0 beta. Pick where your data lives from inside the app, and the apps
finally look like themselves. Nothing to reconfigure — download and open.

---

## Choose the data folder in Setup

**Setup → Data Folder** shows where teams, rosters and games are kept, and lets
you move them. Point it at a Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox folder and the
season syncs to the cloud as you go.

- **Already have data there** from an earlier version or another computer? It is
  used as it is. **Check Folder** tells you what it found — *2 teams, 14 games* —
  before anything changes, and nothing is ever copied over existing data.
- **Starting a new folder?** Tick **Copy** and your current teams and games come
  with you.
- The switch happens the next time you open OpenStatsEngine.

This is what makes a synced folder usable with the Mac app, which cannot be
given `--data` by double-clicking it.

**If the folder is missing at startup** — Google Drive not running yet, say —
OpenStatsEngine waits up to 30 seconds for it, then stops and tells you, rather
than starting on an empty folder and splitting your season in two.

**One computer at a time** against a synced folder: two servers writing to it
make the sync app create conflict copies.

## An icon

The apps, the browser tab and a tablet's home screen now carry the
OpenStatsEngine icon. On Windows the `.exe` also says *OpenStatsEngine* in its
Properties and in Task Manager, instead of *Node.js*.

## Also

- The vMix tab shows the real path of the XML files, wherever the data folder is.
- Releases now publish themselves when the version changes, with the apps
  attached — no more waiting on a hand-made release.
